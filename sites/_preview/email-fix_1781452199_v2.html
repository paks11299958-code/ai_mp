어디가 어떻게 바뀌었는지 한눈에 비교해보세요. 모든 변경 사항에는 이유가 있습니다.

Before — 원본

자동 감지
Dear Sarah,

I want to ask you about the project. I think we need to discuss the deadline because it is little difficult for me to finish until Friday.

Please reply me as soon as possible.

Thanks you.

After — 교정

비즈니스 격식
Dear Sarah,

I'd like to follow up on the project. I believe we should discuss the deadline, as it will be challenging for me to complete the work by Friday.

Could you get back to me at your earliest convenience?

Thank you,
— 이렇게 바뀌었어요
  • · "want to ask" → "I'd like to follow up" (더 정중한 비즈니스 도입부)
  • · "reply me" → "get back to me" (전치사 'to' 누락 → 자연스러운 동사구로)
  • · "Thanks you" → "Thank you" (맞춤법 + 마침 표현 격상)
